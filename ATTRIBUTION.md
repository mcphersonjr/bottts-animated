## Artwork

Face parts (eyes, mouth, textures) are a remix of **Bottts-Neutral**:

- Style: [DiceBear Bottts-Neutral](https://www.dicebear.com/styles/bottts-neutral/)
- Original art: [Bottts](https://bottts.com/) by **Pablo Stanley**
- Art license: **free for personal and commercial use** ([bottts.com](https://bottts.com/))

Paths in `src/parts/` were extracted from the DiceBear Bottts-Neutral style so the faces match the official set.

This package does not claim ownership of Pablo Stanley’s Bottts artwork.
